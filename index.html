🎣 Phishing Fraud

Fake websites or emails designed to steal login credentials and personal data.

💳 Payment Fraud

Fake sellers, UPI scams, card fraud, and unauthorized transactions.

💼 Job Scams

Fake job offers asking for registration fees, personal documents, or crypto payments.

📈 Investment Scams

Fake trading platforms, crypto schemes, and guaranteed-return investment fraud.

❤️ Romance Scams

Fraudsters build fake relationships to request money, gift cards, or crypto transfers.

👤 Identity Theft

Misuse of your name, photos, documents, or personal information.
